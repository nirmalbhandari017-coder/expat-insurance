Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

At Expat Protect Hub ("we," "our," or "us"), we are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your personal information responsibly.

When you request a quote, submit an application, or contact us, we may collect:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number and/or WhatsApp number
  • Date of birth
  • Nationality and country of residence
  • Travel information
  • Insurance requirements
  • Any other information you voluntarily provide

How We Use Your Information

We use your personal information to:

  • Provide insurance quotes and policy information
  • Process applications
  • Contact you regarding your enquiry or policy
  • Provide customer support
  • Send important updates relating to your application or insurance
  • Contact you by phone, SMS, WhatsApp, or email regarding your enquiry and, where permitted by law, relevant products or services that may be of interest to you

You can opt out of marketing communications at any time.

Data Storage and Security

Your information is stored securely using industry-standard security measures. Access to your personal data is limited to authorised personnel who require it to perform their duties.

We retain your information only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes outlined in this policy or to comply with legal and regulatory requirements.

Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal information.

We may share your data with:

  • Insurance providers and underwriting partners
  • Trusted service providers who help us operate our business
  • Legal or regulatory authorities where required by law

All third parties are required to protect your personal information appropriately.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your personal data
  • Restrict or object to certain processing
  • Withdraw consent where applicable

To exercise these rights, please contact us using the details below.
