Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 June 2026

Expat Protect Hub ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This policy explains what we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

  • Contact details you provide via our forms (name, email, phone/WhatsApp).
  • Profile details for quote matching (age, nationality, country of residence).
  • Tracking parameters (page source, UTM tags) for ad-campaign attribution.
  • Standard server logs (IP, browser type) for security and analytics.

2. How we use your information

  • To prepare and send personalised insurance quotes or guides.
  • To contact you by email, phone, or WhatsApp regarding your enquiry.
  • To analyse marketing performance and improve our service.
  • To comply with legal and regulatory obligations.

3. Sharing your information

We share your details only with the insurance providers required to prepare your quote, and with our email and analytics processors. We do not sell your data to third parties.

4. Data retention

We retain your information for as long as needed to provide our service, or as required by law. You can request deletion at any time.

5. Your rights

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your personal data by emailing info@expatprotecthub.com. You can also unsubscribe from marketing emails with one click.

6. Cookies

We use essential cookies for site functionality and analytics cookies (Google Analytics, Meta Pixel) to measure ad performance. You can disable cookies in your browser settings.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email info@expatprotecthub.com.
