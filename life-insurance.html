Common Questions

Life insurance for expats, explained

How much life cover do I actually need?

A common rule is 3–4× your annual income, plus any outstanding mortgage and expected education costs for your children. Our licensed advisors can walk you through it — there’s no cost or obligation.

What if I move to another country?

Your cover follows you. Unlike most domestic life policies, this plan is designed for globally mobile clients — a change of country of residence does not invalidate the policy.

How is the premium calculated?

Premiums are based on your age, gender, smoker status and the sum assured you choose. Our online quote tool uses the current rate table directly — so the price you see is the price you pay.
