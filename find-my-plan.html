Common Questions

Everything you need to know

Is this quiz really free?

Yes. Completely free. We’re paid by the insurer if you eventually buy a policy — you pay the same premium either way.

What happens after I submit my answers?

A licensed advisor reviews your profile and sends 2–3 plan options to your email within 24 hours. You can compare them on your own time — or reply with questions.

Will I be hassled with phone calls?

No. We email first. If you want to chat, just reply or book a call at your convenience. No pressure, no cold calls.

What insurers do you compare?

We work with all major international medical insurers — including Cigna, Allianz, AXA, IMG, Regency, and Bupa Global, among others.

How is my data used?

Your answers are used to match you with the right plan — nothing more. We never sell or share your data. You can request deletion at any time.
