A Quick Preview

The 7 mistakes — at a glance

Each mistake is explained in detail with real examples and a clear fix in the free guide.

01

Choosing local cover for a global life

Country-specific policies leave gaps the moment you travel. A single hospital stay abroad can wipe out years of premiums.

02

Underinsuring the annual limit

US$250K sounds like a lot — until you need 6 weeks in ICU. The right limit depends on where you live, not what you can afford this month.

03

Forgetting medevac & repatriation

An air ambulance from Bali to Singapore can cost US$50,000. Always check if evacuation is included — up to your full plan limit.

04

Hiding pre-existing conditions

Honesty is cheaper than denied claims. Learn what insurers really check — and how good disclosure actually protects you.

05

Ignoring the hospital network

If your insurer doesn’t have direct billing at the hospitals you’d use, you’ll be paying upfront and chasing reimbursement for months.

06

Missing renewal guarantees

The cheapest plan today can refuse to renew tomorrow if you fall sick. Always check “continuation” and “guaranteed renewal” clauses.

07

Buying from a non-licensed agent

The wrong advice costs more than the wrong policy. Always verify your broker is licensed in international medical insurance.
