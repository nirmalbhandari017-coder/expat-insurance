About Us

Expat Protect Hub

We help expats find suitable international health and life insurance options before and during their move abroad.

Living overseas is exciting, but arranging the right protection can feel confusing. Different countries, hospitals, insurers, benefits, exclusions, and pricing can make it difficult to know which plan is right for you. Our goal is to make that process simpler, clearer, and more personal.

We connect individuals, families, retirees, and professionals with expat insurance solutions designed for life abroad. Whether you need health insurance for hospital treatment, outpatient care, emergency support, dental benefits, mental health support, evacuation cover, or life insurance protection, we help guide you toward options that match your needs, location, and budget.

Why Expats Choose Us

Every expat has different needs. A retiree in Thailand, a family relocating to Europe, and a remote worker moving across Asia may all require different levels of cover.

That is why we do not believe in a one-size-fits-all approach. We help you look at important details such as your age, country of residence, medical needs, budget, hospital access, and preferred level of protection.

Our aim is to make insurance easier to understand, so you can make a confident decision before unexpected medical costs happen.
