THE PROBLEM
Loyalty doesn't pay off in expat insurance.
If you've been with the same insurer for more than two years, there's a good chance your plan no longer fits your life. Your situation changes — where you live, who you're covering, how often you use it. Your premium, meanwhile, just quietly goes up.
Most expats haven't reviewed their cover in over two years — and many are paying for benefits they've never once used.

BEFORE YOU RENEW
Five things worth checking.

Your deductible
Has your tolerance for out-of-pocket costs changed since you first signed up?

4
Your unused benefits

Your renewal price
