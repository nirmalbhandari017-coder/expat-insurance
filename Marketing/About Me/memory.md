## Active project: Expat Protect Hub (marketing)

A lead-generation brand for expat insurance, feeding enquiries to Regency for Expats. Pre-revenue. Full detail lives in the brand files in the Marketing folder.

### Quick facts (established 24 May 2026)

- **What it is:** lead-gen brand for expat insurance — connects expats to a real adviser.
- **Known for / wants to be known for:** trusted human help (a real adviser actually talks to people).
- **Differentiator:** real human expertise, backed by Regency advisers.
- **Promise:** peace of mind — "I'm properly protected, whatever happens."
- **Revenue model:** commission from insurers. Free to the customer. Pre-revenue right now.
- **Regency link:** Hub stands on its own; Regency stays in the background in messaging.
- **#1 priority this quarter:** set up the funnel (site, forms, hand-off to Regency). Then traffic/SEO, brand & trust, first enquiries.
- **Primary audience:** established expats (already abroad, already insured, unsure they're getting fair value).
  - Pain: overpaying / unclear cost.
  - Trigger to act: renewal or a price hike.
  - Values most: trust & real advice, plus ease & speed.
  - Main objection: "Is switching even worth it?" (inertia).
- **Secondary audiences:** new/soon-to-be expats, retirees abroad, remote workers & families.
- **Market scope:** global, any nationality.
- **Voice:** warm & reassuring, expert & trustworthy, friendly & conversational; polished & professional but human. Never salesy/pushy or cold/corporate.
