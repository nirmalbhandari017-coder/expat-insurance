# Brand Context — Expat Protect Hub

*Last updated: 24 May 2026*

This file is the source of truth for what Expat Protect Hub is, who's behind it, and where it's heading. Read it before writing anything for the brand.

## In one line

Expat Protect Hub helps expats get the right insurance by connecting them with a real human expert — not a faceless price-comparison machine.

## What it does

Expat Protect Hub is a lead-generation brand for expat insurance. People come to the site looking for cover, and the Hub puts them in front of a real adviser who helps them find the right policy. "Lead generation" here doesn't mean harvesting details and selling them on. It means connecting expats to genuine, human advice — which is exactly what sets it apart from the comparison sites and form-fillers it competes with.

## What it's known for (or wants to be)

Trusted human help. When an expat reaches out, a real adviser actually talks to them — by call, email or message. That human contact is the heart of the brand and the thing every piece of marketing should reinforce.

## What makes it different

Real human expertise. The Hub is backed by Regency advisers who genuinely understand expat cover and its edge cases. Most rivals offer a robo-comparison or a quote form and then disappear. The Hub offers a person who knows what they're talking about.

## The promise

Peace of mind. Every visitor should walk away feeling: *"I'm properly protected, whatever happens."* That feeling is the emotional core of the brand.

## How it makes money

Commission from insurers. When an expat takes out a policy through the Hub (arranged via Regency), the Hub earns a commission. There is no charge to the customer for the advice.

## Growth stage

**Pre-revenue.** The brand is still being built. There's no income yet, so the focus is on laying solid foundations rather than optimising or scaling.

## Priorities this quarter

The single most important thing this quarter is to **set up the funnel** — get the site, enquiry forms and hand-off to Regency working smoothly end to end. Nothing else pays off until this works, so it comes first.

Supporting goals, in rough order once the funnel is solid:

1. **Build traffic / SEO** — get the right expats finding the site through search and content.
2. **Brand & trust** — establish credibility so visitors feel safe enquiring.
3. **First enquiries** — turn visitors into real insurance enquiries and prove the model.

*Note: all four felt urgent at first, but for a pre-revenue brand, trying to do everything at once usually means none of it moves. The funnel is the unblocker — get it right and the rest has somewhere to land.*

## Relationship with Regency

Expat Protect Hub stands on its own as a brand. Regency for Expats is the expertise and advice engine behind it, but stays in the background in customer-facing messaging. Lead with the Hub. (If trust ever needs reinforcing later, Regency can be cited for credibility — but that's not the default for now.)

## The market

Global. The Hub serves expats of any nationality, anywhere in the world. (See `ideal-customer-profile.md` for who to prioritise first.)
