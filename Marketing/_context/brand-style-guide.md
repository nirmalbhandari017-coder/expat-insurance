## 1. Brand Overview

**Expat Protect Hub** is a lead-generation insurance brand for globally mobile people. It connects expats to real human advisers — the antithesis of faceless comparison sites and robo-quote forms.

**Core promise:** Peace of mind. *"I'm properly protected, whatever happens."*

**Personality in one line:** A warm, expert friend who happens to know expat insurance inside out — reassuring, straight-talking, and never salesy.

**Visual personality:** Premium but approachable. Clean, spacious, and confident. Trust-signals are baked into every design choice — the palette, the typography, the whitespace. Nothing cluttered. Nothing pushy.

### Colour Usage Rules

- **Dark slides:** `#0F2A47` or `#1A3A5C` background — white text — teal accents only.
- **Light slides:** `#CFFAFE` or `#F0F7FF` background — `#0F2A47` text — teal dividers and borders.
- **Split slides (cover, CTA):** Left panel teal (`#0891B2`), right panel navy (`#0F2A47`). Invert for variation.
- **Teal is the action colour.** Use it for every interactive or structural cue: numbered steps, CTA buttons, divider lines, card borders.
- **Red is for friction/problems only.** Don't use it decoratively.
- **Never use generic blue (`#4472C4`) from Office defaults.** Always override with brand teal.

## 3. Typography

### Font Stack

| Role | Font | Weight | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Display / Heading | Calibri / Calibri Light | Bold (700) | All major headlines. Use sentence case with trailing period for impact. |
| Body | Calibri | Regular (400) | All body copy, captions, descriptions |
| Label / Eyebrow | Calibri | Regular (400) | Small uppercase, wide letter-spacing (0.15–0.2em). Used for section tags. |

> **Web/social equivalent pairing:** Inter Bold (headings) + Inter Regular (body). Or Poppins Bold + Inter Regular for warmer feel.

### Type Scale (carousel-optimised)

| Level | Size | Weight | Style | Example use |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Display | 38–48pt | Bold | Sentence case + period | Cover / hook slide headline |
| H1 | 28–34pt | Bold | Sentence case + period | Slide main headline |
| H2 | 20–24pt | Bold | Sentence case | Card titles, step titles |
| Eyebrow | 8–10pt | Regular | ALL CAPS, tracked wide | Section label top-left |
| Body | 12–14pt | Regular | Sentence case | Description text |
| Caption | 10pt | Regular | Sentence case | Footnotes, URLs, metadata |
| Stat / Big Number | 48–72pt | Bold | Numerals only | Stat callout slides |

### Typography Rules

- **Headlines end with a period.** "We Make Your Insurance Easy." — this is a brand signature. Apply consistently.
- **Section labels (eyebrows) are ALL CAPS with wide tracking.** Small, positioned top-left. Examples: "THE PROBLEM", "WHO WE ARE", "HOW IT WORKS".
- **Left-align body text.** Centre only stats and CTA elements.
- **No italic for headlines.** Italic is used sparingly for website URLs or pull-quote accents only.
- **Stat numbers pair with small lowercase labels** directly below (e.g. "30,000+" above "Happy Customers").

## 4. Voice and Tone Guidelines

### The voice in a sentence
A warm, expert friend who knows expat insurance inside out. Reassuring. Straight-talking. Never salesy.

### Three defining traits
**Warm and reassuring** — Takes the worry out of a confusing topic. Calm, kind, on the reader's side.

**Expert and trustworthy** — Accurate, specific, and honest. The recommendation is in the reader's genuine interest.

**Friendly and conversational** — Sounds like a person, not a policy document. Plain words. Write the way people speak.

### Tone for carousels specifically
- Hook slides: Conversational question or bold truth. "Living abroad? Here's what your insurer isn't telling you."
- Content slides: Plain, factual, empathetic. One idea per slide.
- CTA slides: Low-pressure. "Free to check. No obligation." Never "Act now!"

### Avoid
- Jargon: indemnity, underwriting, exclusions (without plain explanation)
- Hype: unbeatable, guaranteed, seamless, hassle-free
- Pressure: "don't miss out", "act now", "before prices rise"
- Corporate speak: "leverage", "navigate", "robust", "solutions"

## 5. Visual Identity Rules

### The Split Panel
The brand's signature layout. Two equal vertical panels — one teal, one navy (or the reverse). Used for cover and CTA slides. Decorative circle outlines (partial, half-cropped off the edges) float in the corners as a recurring motif. Never use this layout for content slides.

### The Teal Divider Line
A short, thick teal horizontal rule placed immediately beneath the main headline on every light-background content slide. 80–120px wide, 3–4px thick. This is a brand constant — it grounds the heading and acts as a visual anchor.

### Section Labels (Eyebrows)
Every content slide has a small section label top-left: ALL CAPS, wide tracking, 8–10pt, in mid-navy (`#1A3A5C`) or teal. Always above the main headline, with generous space between them.

### Accent Strips
Light-background slides have a thin teal strip at the very top edge and bottom edge of the slide (3–5px). This frames the slide and adds brand consistency without visual noise.

### Card Components
Bordered cards with a teal outline (`#0891B2`, 1–2pt), white or pale fill, rounded corners (optional, 4–8px). Used for feature grids, step breakdowns, stat displays. Cards never use heavy drop shadows — the border is enough.

### Numbered Step Circles
Filled teal circles (`#0891B2`) with white bold numerals inside. Connected by short horizontal teal dashes between them. Used exclusively for process/steps slides.

### Decorative Circles
Large circle outlines (unfilled, white or teal stroke) that bleed off the edges of dark slides. Used only on dark panels — never on light slides. They soften the geometry without adding literal content.

### What to avoid
- Plain white backgrounds with no colour context
- Clip art, stock icon packs that look generic
- Drop shadows heavier than 10% opacity
- Gradient backgrounds (the brand uses flat colour only)
- Mixed alignment — choose left-aligned or centred per slide and commit

## 6. Logo Usage

**Brand name format:** "EXPAT" in bold uppercase + "PROTECT HUB" in regular tracked uppercase below it on a new line. The word "EXPAT" carries more visual weight (bold). The combination reads as a two-part lockup.

**On dark backgrounds:** White wordmark.
**On teal panels:** White wordmark.
**On light backgrounds:** Deep Navy (`#0F2A47`) wordmark.

**Minimum clear space:** At least the height of the letter "E" on all sides.

**Never:** Stretch, recolour to red/orange, place on busy photographic backgrounds without a solid panel behind it.

## 7. Imagery & Icons

No photography was used in the brand deck — the design is deliberately typography and geometry-led. If photography is introduced:
- Use images of real people in real expat environments (not stock smiling-at-camera poses)
- Treat images with a dark navy or teal overlay at 30–50% opacity to maintain brand cohesion
- Never place uncoloured stock photography directly on slide backgrounds

**Icons:** Simple, line-style or filled single-colour. Teal on light backgrounds, white on dark backgrounds. Place inside small teal-filled circles for feature lists (consistent with the step-circle motif).
