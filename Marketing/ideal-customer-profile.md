# Ideal Customer Profile — Expat Protect Hub

*Last updated: 24 May 2026*

Who we're writing for. The Hub serves expats of any nationality, anywhere in the world — but we can't speak to everyone at once. This file sets the **primary** person to win first, with secondary audiences noted underneath.

## Primary target: the established expat

Someone already living abroad who has insurance, but isn't sure it's still the right deal. They're not new to expat life — they've got a home, maybe a family, a routine. Insurance is one more thing on the list they'd rather not think about, until something makes them.

### Their situation

- Already living overseas, already has cover (often arranged a while ago and not reviewed since).
- Busy. Not actively obsessed with insurance — it surfaces when prompted.
- Has been burned or underwhelmed by faceless providers before, or knows someone who has.

### Their biggest frustration

**Overpaying, with no clarity on cost.** Premiums creep up year after year and they've no real idea whether they're getting fair value or quietly being overcharged.

### What makes them start looking

**Renewal or a price hike.** The premium jumps, the renewal email lands, and they think "right, time to actually check this." That moment is our window — our content and offers should be there to catch people at renewal.

### What they value most when choosing

1. **Trust and real advice.** Someone honest guiding them, not just selling. They want to feel the recommendation is genuinely in their interest.
2. **Ease and speed.** Sorted quickly, without endless forms or hassle. They're busy and low on patience for admin.

Get both right — honest human advice, delivered without friction — and you've matched exactly what this person wants.

### Their biggest hesitation

**"Is switching even worth it?"** Inertia. The effort of changing feels bigger than the likely gain. They half-suspect they're overpaying but aren't sure the hassle pays off.

**How we beat it:** make the case that checking is quick and free, that a real person does the heavy lifting, and that the saving (or the peace of mind from better cover) is worth a ten-minute conversation. Lower the perceived effort and quantify the upside.

### How to win them — messaging that lands

- Catch them at renewal: "Premium gone up? Worth a second look."
- Promise honesty: we'll tell you straight if your current cover is already fine.
- Lead with the human: a real adviser, not a form.
- Kill the hassle objection: quick, free, no pressure, we do the digging.

## Secondary audiences

These matter and we'll serve them, but they're not the first priority. Note them so copy doesn't accidentally exclude them.

- **New / soon-to-be expats** — about to move abroad, sorting cover for the first time. High intent and actively searching; a strong second focus once the funnel proves out.
- **Retirees abroad** — older expats who've retired overseas. Higher-value health needs; they especially value reassurance and a human to talk to.
- **Remote workers and families** — younger movers, digital nomads, families relocating together. A growing, digital-first group.

## Market scope

Global, any nationality. No single-region or single-nationality focus for now.
