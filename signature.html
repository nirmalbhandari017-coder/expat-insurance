3Paste it into Gmail

  1. In Gmail (desktop, in Chrome), click the gear icon top-right → See all settings.
  2. Stay on the General tab and scroll down to the Signature section.
  3. Click + Create new, name it “Expat Protect Hub”, and click Create.
  4. Click inside the empty signature box, then press Ctrl + V (Windows) or ⌘ + V (Mac) to paste. The full brand block will appear with formatting intact.
  5. Under Signature defaults, set your new signature as the default for NEW EMAILS USE and ON REPLY/FORWARD USE.
  6. Scroll to the bottom and click Save Changes. Done — open a new email to confirm.

Tip: if a colleague can’t see the logo, make sure they clicked Copy Signature (rich HTML) not Copy HTML source (raw code).
